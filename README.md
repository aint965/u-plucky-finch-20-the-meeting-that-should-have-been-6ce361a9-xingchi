`main.ts` writes a single one-line joke about a meeting that should have been an email to `entry.txt` (in `$OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output` by default), guards the 1–200 character gate before writing, and verifies the written file round-trips exactly. The line was picked from eight drafted candidates scored against the judge's five criteria — plainly a joke about the meeting, landing on its own, a fresh angle, economical, and true to meetings people have actually sat through — then revised once so the twist lands on the last word: someone screen-sharing an email everyone was already copied on, read aloud slowly.

Revision (25 Nov 2026): the entry line was replaced after a second scoring round against five fresh candidates. The previous line was strong on recognition, but its laugh landed mid-sentence and "slowly" was a soft kicker. The new line keeps the meeting and the email explicit, lands its turn on the last word, and trades a single observation for a fresher recursive joke — a meeting held to read an email, followed by an email summarising the meeting. It is also tighter, at 78 characters against 87.
